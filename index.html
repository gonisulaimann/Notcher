Honest answers

FAQ

Why does macOS warn me on first launch?

The build is ad-hoc signed — there's no Developer ID in the build setup yet — so macOS can't verify it. Right-click → Open → Open once and you're in for good.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes — it falls back to a floating capsule at the top of the screen. Same island, no housing required.

What about multiple displays?

Main display only, for now. A deliberate scope cut while the single-screen experience earns its keep.

Where do files from my iPhone land?

~/Downloads/Notcher Inbox — and they auto-park in the Harbor shelf so they're one hover away.

Does it send anything to a server?

No. Everything travels over your local Wi-Fi, sealed with a key derived from your pairing code. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry — that's the brand, not a footnote.

Is the iPhone app out?

The companion ships separately and hasn't been hardware-tested yet — the repo says so plainly. The Mac's iPhone Link section already shows your pairing code for the day it pairs.
