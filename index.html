Honest answers

FAQ

Why does macOS warn me on first launch?

The build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized — there is no Developer ID in the build environment yet. Right-click → Open → Open once, and it launches normally afterwards.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes — it falls back to a floating capsule at the top of the screen.

What about multiple displays?

Main display only, for now. A deliberate scope cut, not an oversight.

Where do files from my iPhone go?

~/Downloads/Notcher Inbox on the Mac, and they auto-park in the Harbor shelf.

Does it send anything to a server?

No. Everything travels over your local Wi-Fi, sealed with a key derived from your pairing code. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.

Is the iPhone app out?

The companion ships separately — build notes are in the repo README. The Mac's iPhone Link section shows your pairing code whenever you're ready.
